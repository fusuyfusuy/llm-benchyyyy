# multi-step-inventory-word-problem

**dimension(s):** raw model
**difficulty tier:** easy

## Instruction

> A warehouse starts with 480 units of a part. On Monday it ships out 3 orders of 40
> units each and receives a restock of 150 units. On Tuesday it ships out 2 orders of 65
> units each. A quality audit on Wednesday finds 12 units are defective and must be
> removed from stock. How many units remain at the end of Wednesday?

Answer with just the final number, then a one-line explanation of the arithmetic.

## Environment/setup

None — single-turn text prompt, no tools, no files.

## Constraints

- No tool use (calculator/code execution) — this task is meant to probe raw arithmetic
  reasoning without offloading to a tool. If the harness under test always reaches for
  a code-execution tool by default, that's worth noting in `scaffold_notes`, not
  something to suppress.
